EVOQUEST

CURSOR EVOLUTION ARENA

You begin as a speck of Plankton. Eat, evolve, survive.

  1. Move — your creature walks toward your cursor.
  2. Boost — hold left click or Spacebar for a speed burst.
  3. Eat — floor food is 0.2 XP and nudges your boost. You may eat anything below your rank.
  4. Hunt — killing another creature gives you 30% of everything it ever earned, plus most of your boost.
  5. Survive — if you are eaten you keep 50% of all your XP, lifetime total included.
